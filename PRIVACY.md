# Privacy Policy - Binaural Companion

Effective date: August 23, 2026

Binaural Companion is an independent Android audio prototype published by Calogero Turco.

## Data collection

Binaural Companion does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, microphone access, audio-capture permission, or network permission.

Optional before-and-after ratings for calm, focus, and energy are stored only in the app's private local storage on the user's Android device. They are not transmitted to the developer or any third party. Users can delete these records at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## Spotify and other audio applications

The app does not access, record, intercept, download, or modify audio from Spotify or another media application. Android mixes the locally generated stereo layer with other media playing on the device. If Music assist is enabled, the app checks only whether another media playback configuration is active; it does not read the app identity, track metadata, waveform, or listening history.

## Health and safety

Binaural Companion is a wellness and educational audio experience. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date and repository version will be updated before a new public release.

## Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the public GitHub repository's issue tracker.
